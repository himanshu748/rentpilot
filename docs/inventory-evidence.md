## What would prove usable real inventory

Obtain a lister or operator's reply tied to one specific room. Ask for:

1. Current vacancy and the private/shared/whole-unit arrangement.
2. Exact neighbourhood and a location that can be checked independently.
3. Monthly rent, maintenance, utilities, deposit and brokerage separately.
4. Whether a bed, working cooler and LPG cylinder are included; who pays for refills.
5. A current viewing opportunity. A reply alone does not verify identity or ownership.
6. Written permission to use the lister's own listing page/content in RentPilot. Owner permission does not automatically permit scraping a third-party platform; use a page the owner controls or an explicitly licensed feed.

Suggested inquiry, not sent:

> Is the advertised room still available? I am looking for a private room near Bithauli/Bhitauli with monthly rent up to INR 8,000. Please confirm the area, rent and other charges, and whether a bed, cooler and LPG cooking cylinder are included. Could I view it before deciding? Separately, would you permit RentPilot to display your own listing facts and contact route with attribution for a hackathon demo? Please confirm the scope in writing; no assumption of permission will be made.
