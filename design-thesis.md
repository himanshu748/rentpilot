# RentPilot interface thesis

## Audience and job

The primary user is an active renter managing several possible rooms under time pressure. They need to decide which option deserves attention, preserve source evidence and contact a landlord safely.

## Desired feeling

Calm control. The user should understand the state of the search within five seconds and trust why the strongest pursuit is ranked first.

## Archetype

Primary: operational console. Secondary: human service. The interface is a working cockpit, not a marketing dashboard.

## Composition

Desktop uses three functional zones: search context, pursuit queue and selected evidence. Mobile recomposes into a focused pursuit list with details in a sheet. Information density is earned through alignment, typographic hierarchy and hairlines instead of nested cards.

## Primary action

Review the strongest pursuit and send a human-approved inquiry. The action is visible but disabled until the recipient, subject, body and AgentMail credentials are valid.

## Anti-patterns

No gradient bento, fake charts, vanity metrics, generic property photography, excessive pills, floating decoration or automatic email claims.
