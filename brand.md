# RentPilot brand system

RentPilot is a calm rental operations desk for people who are tired of reopening tabs, losing context and wondering what to do next.

## Product truth

Every promising room becomes a pursuit with traceable evidence, a clear status and one safe next action.

## Voice

- Calm, direct and specific
- Helpful without pretending certainty
- Operational language over sales language
- Honest labels for demo data, missing evidence and disabled actions

## Visual direction

The interface combines a rental case file with a transit control desk. Warm paper surfaces hold dense, legible information. Ink navy carries structure. Sage communicates healthy progress. Vermilion is reserved for the next meaningful action.

## Typography

Geist Sans is the working face for clarity and compact information. Geist Mono is used for times, identifiers and evidence labels. Headlines are restrained, left aligned and close to the content they describe.

## Motion

Motion explains state changes. A pursuit rail advances after a valid transition. New evidence reveals with a short masked rise. Draft approval compresses into a send receipt. Decorative looping motion is excluded. Reduced-motion preferences remove transforms and use immediate state changes.

## Imagery

No decorative photography. Listing photos may appear only when they are evidence from a permitted source. Empty image slots are replaced with useful metadata, never generic placeholders.
